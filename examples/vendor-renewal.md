## Decision

Renew the current provider for one year.

## Why now

Switching providers would put a committed delivery date at risk. Keep that delivery commitment while assessing whether a migration can be ready before the next renewal.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Reason selected or rejected |
|---|---|
| Renew for one year | Selected to avoid putting the committed delivery date at risk. |
| Switch providers now | Rejected for the current renewal because of the migration risk. Revisit before the next renewal. |

The source example supplies no separate “do nothing” option or cost analysis. Neither is invented here.

## Roles and approval rule

- Driver: Procurement lead.
- Required approver: Procurement lead.
- Consulted: Delivery owner, on migration readiness and the delivery commitment.
- Informed: Procurement and delivery teams.
- Approval rule for this fictional example: the procurement lead approves the renewal term and its recorded rationale. A later change in that term requires a separate review and approval record.

## Goal, affected records and work

- Serves: Keep the committed release date.
- Affects: The current provider renewal and the timing of a possible migration.
